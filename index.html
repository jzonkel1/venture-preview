Questions, answered

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Venture Plumbing is owned by a Texas Master Plumber (License #44282) and we carry insurance. We handle both residential and commercial work.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Rockport and serve the whole Coastal Bend — Rockport, Fulton, Lamar, Holiday Beach, Aransas Pass, Port Aransas, Ingleside, Portland and Corpus Christi. Not sure if you're in range? Just call.

Do you give upfront pricing?

Always. We diagnose the issue and quote the price before any work begins — the price we quote is the price you pay.

Can you really replace a valve without shutting off my whole building?

Yes. Our RIDGID pipe-freezing machine freezes one section of a live line so we can add or replace valves while the rest of the system keeps running — ideal for hotels and commercial properties.

What is a H.A.L.O. water system?

H.A.L.O. is a certified whole-home water filtration system for cleaner, better-tasting water at every tap. We also install reverse-osmosis drinking systems and water softeners.

Do you work on water heaters and pool heaters?

Yes — water heater replacement and flushing, plus gas-line work for pool heaters and other appliances (we're licensed for natural gas).
