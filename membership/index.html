Plan terms in plain language

  • Card on file required. After you sign up, we text you a secure Podium payment link to put a card on file. We never see your full card number. Your plan starts once your card is saved.
  • Auto-renews. Monthly plans renew every month and annual plans renew every year until you cancel.
  • First month free. Applies to monthly plans with code WEB50, for the first 50 online members. After the free month, your regular monthly price applies.
  • Cancel anytime by calling 361-229-5728. Cancellation stops future charges.
  • After-hours and Sunday rates. Calls after hours and on weekends cost more than weekday calls. Standard members pay a lower Sunday labor rate. Premium members pay regular rates through Saturday daytime, and the lowest after-hours and Sunday rates.
  • Repairs are quoted separately. Your plan covers the listed visits and services. Repairs, parts and replacements are quoted before any work, with your member discount applied.
  • No plan prevents every failure. Inspections and maintenance lower the odds of surprises but can't guarantee against all leaks, breakdowns or outages.
  • Service area. Plans cover one home in our service area and transfer to your next home in the area.
