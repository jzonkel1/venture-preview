Non-Invasive Water Shutoff FAQ

Common questions

How can you replace a valve without shutting off the whole building?

Our RIDGID pipe-freezing machine freezes one section of a live line into a plug, so we can add or replace valves while the rest of the system keeps running.

Can every pipe be frozen?

No. Pipe freezing is available for select commercial applications when appropriate. Call 361-229-5728 to ask whether it fits your job.

What kinds of buildings is this for?

Hotels, offices, restaurants and other occupied commercial properties around Rockport and the Coastal Bend, anywhere shutting down large portions of the building isn't an option.

Why not just shut the water off for an hour?

In an occupied hotel, restaurant or office, even a short shutdown can mean guests without water, closed restrooms or a kitchen that can't run. Non-invasive shutoff keeps the rest of the building in service while we work.

How much does pipe freezing cost?

It depends on the pipe, where it is and what work is being done once the line is isolated. We diagnose the job and give you an upfront price before any work begins. Call 361-229-5728.
